# 掌上待办 Logo v1

设计概念：掌心记事，随手完成。以产品从屏幕左侧伸出的手掌为原型，简化五指轮廓，并在掌心加入手写勾。

生成方式：内置 imagegen；原手掌仅作为造型与颜色参考。方案作为独立设计素材保存，未接入应用。

## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: HandTodo / 掌上待办 primary logo symbol, usable as a desktop app icon.
Primary request: Design one polished original logo based on the product's defining interaction: an open palm emerges from the left edge of the screen, users jot small todos directly on their palm and erase them when finished.
Input image: the existing product hand illustration is a reference for its open palm gesture, wrist entering from left, thumb above and four fingers pointing right, warm peach skin and gentle dark brown ink. This is a NEW simplified brand mark, not a replacement illustration.
Subject: one compact open hand, palm facing viewer, wrist entering from lower left, thumb pointing up and four softly rounded fingers extending toward upper right. Exactly five digits, natural silhouette. Shorten and simplify proportions into an exceptionally clear compact logo; the palm should be generously sized. On the palm, one confident slightly irregular dark ink-blue handwritten check mark, as if drawn directly on skin. The check is prominent and legible at small size, unboxed.
Style: sophisticated friendly flat vector-style logo, simple controlled rounded contours, warm pale peach fill, consistent medium dark brown outline, at most one restrained muted terracotta shape for depth. A personal analog desktop utility, quiet and approachable. Optical balance and clean edges. Sparse palm lines only if essential, no fine texture. The check is the focal point.
Composition: a single isolated logo centered in a square canvas with comfortable equal margins; logo occupies around 78% of canvas. Transparent background, no enclosing app tile, no scene, no presentation board, no alternate options, no letters or brand text.
Constraints: one hand only, exactly five fingers; preserve the gesture logic of the reference while radically simplifying it; genuine transparent background. No photorealism, no skin texture, no tiny anatomical details, no realistic nails, no extra objects, no pen or pencil, no paper sheet or clipboard, no checkbox square, no sparkles, no gradients, no 3D, no drop shadow, no watermark.
